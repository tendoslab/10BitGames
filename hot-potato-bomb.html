Hot Potato Bomb

A frantic local multiplayer party game for 2-6 players. Pass the ticking bomb before it explodes — customisable timers, misfire penalties, and full touch-screen support.

How to play

  • 01Gather 2 to 6 players around one screen or keyboard, then set up names, keys, and a fuse duration in Tournament Setup.
  • 02The player holding the bomb must tap their name (or press their assigned key) to pass it on before the timer runs out.
  • 03If the timer hits zero while you're holding the bomb, you're eliminated. Last player standing wins the round — first to 3 round wins takes the tournament.
  • 04Misfire penalty: tapping your name when you don't have the bomb yanks it to you instantly and knocks 1.5 seconds off the fuse. No button mashing!
