Get in touch

We'd love to hear from you! Whether you've found a bug in one of our mini-games, have a great idea for a classic game we should re-imagine next, or just want to say hello, the Mini Games Planet team is always open to feedback.

How to reach us

The best and fastest way to get in touch is via email:

tendoslab@gmail.com

What to include in your email

  • Bug reports: the name of the game, the device/browser you were playing on (e.g. iPhone Safari, Windows Chrome), and a brief description of what went wrong so we can squash it.
  • Game suggestions: got a favorite retro or logic game? Let us know what you want to play next.
  • Copyright & legal: as stated in our Terms and Conditions, if you have a copyright concern or business inquiry, please be as detailed as possible so we can address it promptly.

We read every email and try our best to respond within 24 to 48 hours. Thanks for playing and helping us make Mini Games Planet better!
