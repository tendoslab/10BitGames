How to play

  • 01The game consists of a large 3x3 grid, with each square containing a smaller 3x3 tic-tac-toe board.
  • 02Win a small board by getting 3-in-a-row within it. Once won, the whole square is claimed.
  • 03To win the entire game, you must claim 3 small boards in a row on the large grid.
  • 04The Global Rule: Your move sends your opponent to the corresponding small board. (e.g., Playing in the bottom-left of a small board forces the next player to play in the bottom-left of the large grid).
