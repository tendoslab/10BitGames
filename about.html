About us

The story behind the planet

Welcome to Mini Games Planet!

Created by the Mini Games Planet team, this site started out as a simple passion project. We wanted to build a lighthearted, stress-free corner of the internet where people could drop in, play a few rounds, and kill some time without any hassle.

Our mission

We believe gaming should be accessible and frictionless. That's why our main focus is providing a hub for 100% free games that load instantly, right in your browser. Zero downloads, zero installations, zero sign-ups. You just click and play.

As developers and gamers ourselves, we also have a soft spot for nostalgia. A big part of what we do here involves re-imagining some of our favorite classic game concepts, bringing them back to life with a fresh coat of paint and modern browser mechanics.

Looking ahead

We're constantly coding and brainstorming behind the scenes. Our goal for the future of Mini Games Planet is twofold: keep expanding our catalog with more original mini-games, and build a welcoming community of fellow mini-game lovers.

Whether you're here to beat a high score, challenge a friend, or just relax for five minutes, we're glad you found your way to our orbit.

Thanks for stopping by, and happy gaming!
— The Mini Games Planet Team
