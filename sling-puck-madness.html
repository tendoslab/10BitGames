How to Play

Master the sling
Tap/click a puck on your side to select it.
Drag back, then release to sling it toward the gap in the centre wall.
Pucks bounce off walls and can knock other pucks.
Classic: First player to clear all pucks from their own side wins.
Race: First to push all 5 of your pucks to the opponent's side wins. Be careful — opponents can knock your pucks back!
In 2P mode the board rotates 180° each turn so you always face your pucks.

How to play

  • 01Tap or click a puck on your side to select it, then drag back and release to sling it toward the gap in the centre wall.
  • 02Pucks bounce off the walls and can knock into other pucks — line up your shots carefully.
  • 03Classic mode: be the first to clear every puck off your own side.

  • 05Play solo against the CPU (three difficulty levels) or pass-and-play 2 Player, where the board flips 180° each turn so you always face your own pucks.
